FAQ

Questions? We've got answers

What kind of photo works best?

A clear, well-lit photo with one main subject works best. Full-body or half-body shots facing the camera give the most natural results. Avoid heavily blurred or cropped images.

How long does it take to generate a video?

Most videos are ready in about 30 seconds. During peak times it may take a little longer. Premium members get priority rendering.

Can I use photos of my pets or cartoon characters?

Yes! Dance AI works with people, pets, illustrations and characters. Just make sure you own the image or have permission to use it.

What happens to the photos I upload?

Your photos are used only to generate the videos you request. We never sell your data. You can delete your content and account at any time. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your App Store or Google Play account settings. You can cancel anytime at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

Can I share videos on social media?

Absolutely. Save videos to your gallery or share directly to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, Facebook and messaging apps.
